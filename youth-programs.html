FAQ section

Questions parents usually ask before registering

Answers below are written to be easy to update later as final schedules and age groups are confirmed.

What ages can participate?

Programs are being organized by youth-friendly age group. Final divisions will be shared before registration closes.

Does my child need experience?

No. Both programs are designed to welcome beginners while still helping returning players improve.

Are girls welcome?

Yes. Girls are welcome in both NFL FLAG Football and MLS GO Soccer programs.

What equipment is needed?

Families will receive a simple equipment list before the season. Required items will stay appropriate and parent-friendly.

When will practices and games take place?

Practice and game timing will be released once teams are finalized. Families on the list will get updates first.

Can parents volunteer or coach?

Yes. Parents can express interest in coaching or volunteering during the signup process.

How do I register?

Choose your program, start the registration step above, and complete the interest form if final payment is not yet live.

Are spots limited?

Yes. Team and roster capacity is limited, so families are encouraged to reserve a spot as early as possible.
