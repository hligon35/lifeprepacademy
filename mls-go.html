MLS GO Program

MLS GO gives youth athletes a chance to build confidence, improve core skills, and stay active in a positive team environment. Explore the program experience below and register when your family is ready.

Paducah GO Soccer, locally operated by LifePrep Academy Foundation, gives children ages 4-14 an inclusive, structured, and exciting place to learn the game. With age-appropriate small-sided play, MLS GO coaching resources, official uniforms, and a no-cut approach, every player gets an opportunity to learn, participate, and belong.

If your executed operator agreement confirms designation, this language can be upgraded to Official MLS GO League Operator.

  • Ages 4-14
  • All skill levels welcome

What Families Can Expect

Everyone gets to play

MLS GO has a no-cut approach, and team sizes are designed to provide every player with meaningful time on the field.

Built for development

Age-appropriate small-sided games give players more touches on the ball and more opportunities to learn.

More than the score

For younger divisions, the emphasis is on fundamentals, confidence, teamwork, and enjoying the game, not standings.

The MLS GO experience

Players can receive an official kit with an MLS GO reversible jersey, shorts, and socks. Coaches are supported with age-specific MLS GO curriculum and resources.

The official rulebook supports the no-cut, playing-time, age-format, and development-first statements: MLS GO Rulebook.

Built for New and Developing Players

Whether a child is just getting started or looking to sharpen fundamentals, MLS GO creates a welcoming on-ramp to the game.

Focus: confidence, movement, and teamwork from day one.

Structured Sessions, Real Progress

Program sessions are designed around age-appropriate drills, small-sided play, and positive coaching that keeps kids engaged.

Every session reinforces effort, growth, and sportsmanship.

Community-Centered Experience

Families, coaches, and local partners all play a role in creating a program culture where players feel seen and supported.

Youth development is the mission, soccer is the vehicle.

Our Commitment to Player Safety

Paducah GO Soccer is committed to a positive, inclusive, and safe environment. Coaches, volunteers, and other covered adults are subject to MLS GO screening and training requirements. Program activities follow athlete-protection, emergency-response, and weather-safety procedures.

  • Background screening for coaches, volunteers, officials, staff, and other adults with regular participant access
  • Annual SafeSport training and applicable state training
  • No isolated one-on-one adult/minor interactions outside an observable and interruptible setting
  • A written emergency action plan for each venue
  • AED and first-aid locations documented
  • Coaches educated on emergency response
  • Concussion and cardiac-response protocols
  • Lightning suspension using the 30/30 rule
  • Heat decisions based on heat index, hydration, shade, and cooling access

Publish and refine each safety item as local implementation procedures are confirmed.

Scheduling updates and additional session information are shared through the registration pathway and youth program announcements.
