About Lifeprep Academy Foundation

At LifePrep Academy Foundation, we are dedicated to providing mental health resources, athletic training, financial literacy workshops, and youth advancement opportunities. Our website serves as a platform to raise awareness and facilitate donations, supporting our mission to empower individuals in Baltimore, Maryland.

We focus on practical, hands‑on learning, trusted mentorship, and accessible resources so every participant can take the next step with confidence.

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for underserved communities by promoting both mental and physical health awareness, mentorship and financial literacy.

We pursue this mission by meeting learners where they are, building supportive relationships, and connecting them to clear pathways in school, work, and life.

Our Offerings

Life Prep Academy offers various services that help to drive our mission. If you are interested in any of the services listed below, feel free to reach out to us via email at info@lifeprepacademyfoundation.com or by using the form included in the Contact Us section of this site.

Our Programs

Mental Health Resources

Spreading awareness about the importance of mental health is a top priority of Life Prep Academy. We partner with local and national health organizations to facilitate workshops and provide resources, so that people within the community are empowered and encourage to prioritize their mental health as well. Sessions include coping strategies, stress management, and how to access local support—reducing stigma and building resilience.

Learn more about Mental Health Resources

Athletic Training

Our foundation works with Life Prep Academy LLC to promote health awareness through sports training. We offer scholarships and grants for athletes that are willing to be better but can't afford next level training.

Learn more about Athletic Training

Financial Literacy Workshops

We’ve partnered with financial advisors to facilitate workshops to empower members of our community to make informed financial decisions, navigate financial traumas and to take control of their financial well-being. Participants leave with a simple action plan and tools they can use right away to build long‑term stability.

Learn more about Financial Literacy Workshops

Youth and Community Advancement Opportunities

It is important that we make ourselves accessible to Baltimore City’s Youth and use our resources to aid in their advancement. Life Prep Academy welcomes all requests for community outreach and mentorship opportunities. We connect youth to mentorship, leadership opportunities, community projects, and next‑step pathways in education and careers.

Learn more about Youth & Community Advancement
