Our Founder

Bryan Hall is a former professional American football player who won Super Bowl XLVII with the Baltimore Ravens in 2013. Born and raised in Paducah, Kentucky, Bryan overcame numerous challenges in his early life to pursue his passion for football and achieve success at the highest level.

Now, as the founder of the Life Prep Academy Foundation, Bryan is dedicated to making a lasting impact on youth and communities in both Baltimore, Maryland and Paducah, Kentucky by promoting mental wellness, athletic development, financial literacy, and leadership opportunities.

His journey from perseverance to purpose fuels the Foundation's holistic model—addressing mind, body, and future readiness while creating pathways to stability and leadership.

About LifePrep Academy Foundation

At LifePrep Academy Foundation, we are dedicated to providing mental health resources, athletic training, financial literacy workshops, and youth advancement opportunities. Our website serves as a platform to raise awareness and facilitate donations, supporting our mission to empower individuals in Baltimore, Maryland.

We focus on practical, hands‑on learning, trusted mentorship, and accessible resources so every participant can take the next step with confidence.

Our Mission

To provide opportunities for underserved communities by promoting both mental and physical health awareness, mentorship and financial literacy.

We pursue this mission by meeting learners where they are, building supportive relationships, and connecting them to clear pathways in school, work, and life.

Our Offerings

Life Prep Academy offers various services that help to drive our mission. If you are interested in any of the services listed below, feel free to reach out to us by using the form included on the Contact page. We look forward to working with you!

  • Interactive workshops and school assemblies
  • Small‑group coaching and mentorship circles
  • Team clinics and athletic development sessions
  • Family nights and community engagement events
