Founder

Bryan Hall is a former professional American football

player who won Super Bowl XLVII with the Baltimore

Ravens in 2013. Born and raised in Paducah,

Kentucky, Bryan overcame numerous challenges in

his early life to pursue his passion for football and

achieve success at the highest level. Now, as the

founder of the Life Prep Academy Foundation,

Bryan is dedicated to making a lasting impact on the

youth and communities in both Baltimore, Maryland

and Paducah, Kentucky, by promoting mental

wellness, athletic development, financial literacy,

and leadership opportunities.



Health Awareness: Mind and Body

Mental Health Workshops

Facilitate workshops and seminars to raise awareness about mental health issues, coping strategies, and access to support resources

Physical Health Clinics

Organize health screenings, fitness classes, and sports-related activities to promote physical wellness in the community

Local \& National Partnerships

Collaborate with community organizations, hospitals, and national health groups to expand the reach and impact of health awareness programs

By addressing both mental and physical health, Life Prep Academy Foundation is empowering individuals to prioritize their overall well-being and take proactive steps towards a healthier future.



Financial Literacy: Building a Stable Future

Financial Literacy Workshops

The foundation collaborates with financial advisors to facilitate workshops that empower community members to make informed financial decisions.

Navigating Financial Challenges

The workshops provide resources and strategies to help participants navigate and overcome financial hardships and traumas.

Taking Control of Finances

Participants leave the workshops with a personalized action plan and practical tools to take control of their financial wellbeing and build long-term stability.

By providing accessible financial literacy education and resources, Life Prep Academy Foundation empowers individuals to make informed decisions, achieve financial stability, and secure a brighter future for themselves and their communities.



Opportunities for Underserved Communities

Youth Flag Football Camps

Life Prep Academy Foundation hosts engaging flag football l camps to promote physical activity, teamwork, and leadership development among underserved youth.

Step Shows with Divine 9 Fraternities and Sororities

The foundation partners with historically Black Greek-letter organizations to organize thrilling step show events, celebrating culture and fostering community.

Men's Health Awareness Weekend

This comprehensive weekend event focuses on improving mental wellness, providing resources, and empowering men in the community to prioritize their health and well-being.





Life Prep Academy Foundation is dedicated to creating transformative change in communities by empowering individuals through holistic programming and impactful partnerships. Through their unwavering commitment to mental wellness, athletic development, financial literacy, and youth leadership, the foundation continues to make a lasting difference, inspiring hope and unlocking new possibilities for the people of Baltimore and beyond.
